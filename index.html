Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining and donating to Linda Mwananchi.

Linda Mwananchi is a nationwide campaign in Kenya focused on civic empowerment and accountability. We organize members and mobilize donations to fund programs across all 47 counties.

96% of every donation goes directly into county-level programs, events, and outreach. The remaining 4% covers essential operating costs. We publish a breakdown in The Numbers section above.

Yes. Linda Mwananchi operates as a registered civic campaign in Kenya, and our financial reports are reviewed and published on a regular schedule for full public accountability.

Click "Sign Up" or "Create Free Account" anywhere on this page, enter your email, and confirm your account. Membership is completely free and takes less than a minute.

Members receive early access to local events, monthly transparency reports, a verified badge, and direct updates from their county chapter, all from their personal dashboard after signing in.

We currently accept M-Pesa, major debit and credit cards, and direct bank transfer. All payments are processed securely and you'll receive an instant confirmation.

Yes. You can choose to hide your name from public donor lists during checkout. Your donation will still be recorded internally for our own accountability records.

Every donation generates an automatic email receipt with the amount, date, and reference number, which you can use for your own personal records.

Absolutely. You can volunteer at local events, help organize your county chapter, or simply spread the word. Sign up as a member and select "Volunteer" from your dashboard to get started.

Yes. Kenyans in the diaspora and international supporters are welcome to join as members and donate online from anywhere in the world.

Visit The Numbers section for live totals, or check the Feed for regular updates and stories showing exactly how donations are being put to work on the ground.
